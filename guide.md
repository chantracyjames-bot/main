## How to install my repositories
### Cloning the repository:
- On Android and iOS:
	1. Install _Obsidian_ and _GitSync_ from the Apple App Store or the Google Play Store.
	2. Open _Obsidian_.
	3. Go through the prompts:
		a. Click on "__Create a vault__".
		b. Click on "__Continue without Sync__".
		c. Add a name for your temporary vault.
		d. Click on "__Create a vault__".
		e. Allow necessary permissions.
		f. When prompted, place your vault wherever you want.
		- __Note__: It is recommended to turn off "__Store in _iCloud___", so that the repository locally stays on your device.
	4. Create a temporary file using _Obsidian_, it makes finding the vault a little easier.
	5. Close _Obsidian_ completely.
	6. Open _GitSync_.
	7. Go through the prompts:  
		a. Click on "__Let's Go__".
		b. You can skip the next prompt.
		c. Click on "__Sync Mode__", the on "__Next__".
		d. Click on "__Premium Features__", and the "__Skip__".
		e. Optional, you can either turn notifications on or keep it off.
		f. Allow permissions when needed.
		g. Click on "__Setup__", then "__Use Offline__".
	8. On the "__Clone Remote Repository__" page, either place:
		a. [https://github.com/chantracyjames-bot/cs_notes](https://github.com/chantracyjames-bot/cs_notes)    - Computer Science Notes
		b. [https://github.com/chantracyjames-bot/p_notes](https://github.com/chantracyjames-bot/p_notes)     - Programming Notes
		c. [https://github.com/chantracyjames-bot/programming](https://github.com/chantracyjames-bot/programming) - Programming 
		- __Note__: You can add more repositories later, refer to the "Adding more repositories" section for more information.
	9. Click on either options, "__Nested Clone" is recommended.
	10. Click on "Done" and close _GitSync_.
	11. Open _Obsidian_.
	12. Open the left sidebar, click on your temporary vault name.
	13. Click on "__Manage vaults...__".
	14. Click on "__Open folder as vault".
	15. Browse through your files until you find the cloned repository's folder.
	16. You are now done!
### Updating the repository:
- On Android and iOS
	1. Open _GitSync_.
	2. Click on "__Sync Changes__".
	3. Optional:
		a. Scroll down and look for "__Scheduled Sync Settings__", click on it.
		b. You can set the automated time where the repository auto updates to the latest sources.
		- Note: Setting the automated updated schedule too frequently may drain more battery. 
	4. You are now done!
### Adding more repositories:
- On Android and iOS:
	- A. Repository is not offline or locally stored (remote).
		1. Open _GitSync_.
		2. Click on the "__X__" icon right next to the "__Directory__" bubble, or below the "__X Auth__" icon when you are not authenticated with any Git solutions.
			- __Note__: This does not delete the repositories, they are still stored on you physical device.
		3. The screen should now say "__No Commits Found__" or "__No repo found__".
		4. Click on "__Clone__".
		5. The setup is the same as pulling a new repository, enter any of the other URLs to add to your repository.
		6. You are now done!
		- B. Repository is stored locally or on device.
		1. Open _GitSync_.
		2. Click on the "__X__" icon right next to the "Directory" bubble, or below the "__X Auth__" icon when you are not authenticated with any Git solutions.
			- __Note__: This does not delete the repositories, they are still stored on you physical device.
		3. The screen should now say "__No Commits Found__" or "__No repo found__".
		4. Click on the folder icon right next to the "__Directory__" bubble.
		5. Browse through your files until you find your locally stored repository's folder.
		6. You are now done!
